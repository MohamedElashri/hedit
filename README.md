hedit is a text editor for Linux, built for the work you leave open: logs to
inspect, snippets to revisit, and notes that do not need a filename yet.

## A place for your work

- **Keep your scratchpads.** Name your notes, return to them after restarting,
  and reopen closed buffers from the shelf.
- **Stay with your logs.** Open large files, follow incoming output, and bookmark
  the details worth returning to.
- **Make it comfortable.** Choose a theme or customize your colors, font size,
  and word wrap.
- **Work with CUDA.** Edit highlighted source and launch debugging or memory
  checks from the editor.

## Get started

Follow the [installation guide](USER_GUIDE.md#installation), then open a file or
start a scratchpad with **Ctrl+N**. The [user guide](USER_GUIDE.md) covers everyday
editing, themes, shortcuts, and recovery.

hedit is currently an early release for Linux.
